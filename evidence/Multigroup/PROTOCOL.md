## Deterministic bounded refinement

Use the appendix convex epigraph primal and concave simplex dual D(lambda). No convexity claim for max_g f_g(C(lambda)) on simplex weights. For each partition, form exact quadratic coefficients from N/S/SS and global counts. Start the dual iterate at uniform 1/m. Evaluate that point and all m vertices. At each of at most 12 Frank-Wolfe steps, evaluate the unrounded exact minimizer of the weighted quadratic; choose the smallest-index group with largest exact cost as the vertex direction. For the segment to that vertex, evaluate alpha=0,1 and six dyadic derivative bisection midpoints, moving the lower endpoint on strictly positive derivative, upper on zero/negative. Select largest exact D among those segment evaluations, tie by lexicographically smallest rational lambda. An exactly zero Frank-Wolfe gap terminates; otherwise run all 12 steps, including stalls. Best dual bound is the largest D over every evaluated lambda, tie by lexicographic lambda.

For positive cluster denominator, exact weighted mean is rounded once to binary64 per coordinate. At zero denominator, use the sole occupied group's exact mean when there is exactly one; otherwise retain incumbent (including fully empty clusters). No empty mean is evaluated. Every evaluated candidate has its actual represented fixed-partition objective computed exactly. Choose minimum Phi, tie by lexicographic lambda, then accept iff its exact cost is <= incumbent; otherwise return an exact copy of incumbent. Output gap = returned fixed-partition Phi minus best exact D. Store exact lambda, D, primal and gap, candidate trace, termination and guard result. A finite gap concerns this partition only; no global clustering optimum is claimed.

Fresh and whole-client DIRECT share the same represented refinement loop. DIRECT reuses surviving local summaries and resident encoded/represented arrays, rebuilds normalization/server, and performs all survivor assignments and moments for each T. No certificate heuristics or record deletion service. T0 compact state additionally supports raw-free whole-client reuse and returned compact next-state under a trusted-state contract. No physical erasure, complete-cache equality or adaptive fresh-independent guarantee.
