# Claims and limits

The current claim snapshot is `PAPER_CLAIMS_SNAPSHOT.md`; its page/table numbers refer to the consolidated manuscript snapshot at packaging time. This package supplies experiments, not an alternate manuscript.

- P2/P3: original 810 replay comparisons and unfavorable end-to-end certificate results are retained. Same-map exactness is not a universal speedup claim.
- Compact C0: whole-client deletion uses summaries and persistent IDs under a trusted-state contract. It is T=0 and does not remove the preprocessing influence of deleted records. Summaries and returned dictionaries must not be mutated.
- Matched positive rounds: canonical Direct replay is compared with fresh training at the same T1/T2. Compact C0 ratios cannot be divided by positive-round ratios to obtain measured cross-budget latency.
- Diversity: 30 cases contain the original c0 client in 15 cases. There are two selected clients per dataset, only one new identity per dataset. Reuse is disclosed, not counted as independent evidence.
- Sequences: 15 sequences, three correlated withdrawals each. Buffered persistence is measured; durable storage/fsync is not claimed. Only newly produced local trusted state is unpickled.
- Group quality: maximum group cost, sum of group-normalized costs and population-average cost are different quantities. Adverse individual-group and population changes remain in the tables. Group_Quality reuses existing outputs.
- Matched-start Lloyd: two updates from identical fair-initialized centers, same survivors and assignment conventions; it tests update choice, not initialization or a full conventional-k-means campaign. Exact T2 audit rescoring covered 12 selected output tuples; that prior independent audit did not claim all 150 distinct saved tuples were independently rescored.
- Multi-group: nine categorical RAC1P groups; race remains a feature; the smallest retained group has seven records. One fixed Missouri withdrawal is not broad demographic validation. Exact replay and a bounded optimizer gap are distinct claims. The positive-round prototype is not the canonical binary learner.
- Negative/theoretical checks: scalar fixtures and exact finite enumerations corroborate stated bounded examples and assumptions. They are not machine-checked proofs of the entire paper. The optional Lean bundle verifies only its separately stated finite real-valued model; see `optional/README.md` for missing executable and raw-data bridges.

No raw-to-processed provenance is invented for Adult/Credit. No hidden source dataset, account, original-machine path or private author archive is required for the documented frozen-data commands. Optional raw ACS reconstruction needs public sources and additional storage and was not rerun during packaging.
