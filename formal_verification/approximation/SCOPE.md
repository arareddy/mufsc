## Main dependency boundary

No theorem assumes the desired global warm-start or heterogeneity conclusion as a premise. The direct generic transfer accepts a local replacement bound, but `split_warm_start` and `merged_warm_start` derive it from explicit finite local geometry and per-call literature guarantees. Root geometry, Cauchy–Schwarz, both conditioning steps, conversion to Phi, quantization and exact constants are proved.

The external result is Makarychev et al.'s exact-k k-means++ factor alpha=5(log k+2), as cited in the manuscript. It is passed through hypotheses named `makarychev_local`, `makarychev_server`, or `makarychev_unweighted`; these are functions/inequalities in theorem types, not added logical axioms. Local/server laws and sampled center tuples must satisfy those genuine external guarantees. The full sampler-state specification connecting the generic finite-law reduction to every concrete local/server call is not yet a single instantiated theorem. This prevents claiming unconditional complete formalization of `thm:warmstart` or the entire upper half of `thm:kappa` from only the source code in this bundle.

No statement identifies finite PCG64 seeds with ideal independent bits, proves the production categorical sampler, establishes clipping/encoding/rounding correctness, or verifies empirical measurements. The exact Lean theorem-to-paper dictionary is a reviewed human interpretation, not a kernel-checked fact about natural-language prose.
