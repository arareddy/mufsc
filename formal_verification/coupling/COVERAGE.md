## Model and probability definitions

`oldCategory u=u mod 2`. `newProposal m u` rejects u≥2m−1 and otherwise returns category 0 for u<m and category 1 otherwise. Accepted minimal traces consist of r copies of 2m−1 followed by an accepting u. Their cylinder weight is (1/(2m))^(r+1). This explicit independent-proposal law is normalized and includes unbounded rejection. It does not assert termination for every possible infinite tape: the all-reject tape exists and has zero mass in the model.

Word assembly is a genuine finite equivalence from functions `Fin ell → Fin (2^64)` to integers below `(2^64)^ell`. Uniformity of its masked low bits is counted, including widths above 64. `wordsNeeded w=(w+63)/64` handles zero width, one-word widths, and multiword widths. Discarding unused high bits is part of the proposed interpretation of these blocks.

The first-call event uses the actual six ordered cumulative masses. The narrow joint model uses the same tail proposal for the old low bit and retained sampler. In the wide model the old second bit is fixed by the first block while the retained second call uses a fresh tail. The joint-trace proofs sum those local product laws directly.

## Named remaining obligations

1. **Infinite shared-word execution refinement.** No measure-theoretic construction of an infinite IID 64-bit tape and no refinement theorem from that tape's adaptive word-consumption execution to all of the block/trace laws is included. Fresh disjoint tail blocks have their independent uniform law by the model's cylinder-weight definition. The finite block bijection, width split, operational accepting traces, normalization, and residual-tail limit are proved; their global stream interpretation is an explicit modeling boundary.
2. **Full FTF-1 initialization linkage.** The complete local weighted sampling, persistent-ID completion, server word-offset state machine, and rational denominator-clearing implementation are not extracted from or connected to production code. Local constant-coordinate invariance, record counts, integer primitive reduction, six-slot threshold mapping, and marginal D² table are checked separately. A global refinement must show all these representations coincide with executable FTF-1, including random identities and later-call offsets.
3. **Numerical outward bounds.** Exact real-distance Basic/Runner-up decisions and the abstract sound-certificate rejection consequence are proved. Floating/outward interval arithmetic and the successful B-point Runner-up comparisons for every implemented (b,L) setting are not encoded. Controlled-word fixtures are not treated as proofs.
4. **Actual F0/J0 expectation linkage.** The event contribution and finite/countable expectation inequalities are verified. Applying them to the full unlearning execution still requires the preceding stream/initialization/numerical refinements and the identification of actual instrumentation counts with the model's transactional assignment counts. The generic expectation theorems expose these premises; they do not assume the expected lower bound itself.
5. **Additional upper bounds.** The optional direct cache reconstruction asymptotic upper bound and unrelated appendix work-accounting claims were not formalized in this bundle.

These boundaries prevent a claim that the complete manuscript proposition or production PRNG has been fully kernel-verified. Empirical measurements, manuscript-to-definition correspondence, wall-clock work, and universal impossibility of exact unlearning are not conclusions of this supplement.

## Dependencies and trust

No imported domain-specific or literature theorem is assumed as an axiom. Mathlib supplies standard finite sums, natural gcd arithmetic, real algebra/order, finite encoding equivalences, geometric-series convergence, and summable-series comparison. Lean 4.19.0 and the pinned mathlib commit are recorded in `DEPENDENCIES.json`. Every final module is compiled with trust level 0 and one worker; every named theorem is audited.
